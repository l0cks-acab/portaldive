# Changelog

## 0.1.2
- Also replaces the transition screens other mods add for their dimensions (The Undergarden, Deeper and Darker and others), so every dimension gets the dive. New `replaceModScreens` option, on by default, to keep their screens instead.

## 0.1.1
- The tunnel now leaves in the colour of the dimension you came from and blends into the destination's colour, instead of flashing the old colour for a frame.
- Mod list logo.

## 0.1.0
- First release: hyperspace dive on dimension change, hyper-braking arrival, portal windup, per-dimension tints, sounds, config.
